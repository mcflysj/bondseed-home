What is free?

Tools, worksheets, Today, and Guides are free without an account. The nine-skill communication course has a complete free first chapter; lifetime access unlocks Chapters 2–9.

Who publishes the resources?

BondSeed is the publishing name for this independently maintained parent-child communication project. BondSeed is responsible for its content, references, and corrections.

Read About BondSeed, Sources and Evidence, and the Editorial Policy. Public references support relevant principles; they do not endorse the project or validate every suggested phrase.

How should I use a tool?

Choose one step that fits your family, adapt the words, and leave room for a short answer or another time. Worksheet entries stay in your browser tab. Download or print them before leaving.

The resources offer general communication education. Persistent distress or concerns about safety need qualified local support. See the Privacy Policy for site and account data.
